Frequently Asked Questions

What is the standard life expectancy of Mangalam HDPE pipes?

Our HDPE pipes are manufactured using premium PE100 resins and have a design life exceeding 50 years under standard operating temperatures and pressure ratings.

Are these pipes suitable for high-pressure chemical transmission lines?

Yes, due to inert polymer bonds, they offer excellent resistance to acids, alkalis, and aggressive chemical products up to PN16 pressure ratings limits.
